Gentle Measuring Guide

How To Take Accurate Measurements at Home

Babies rarely stay still for measuring tapes! Here are our gentle, stress-free recommendations for getting the right size.

1. Length / Height While Napping

Lay your baby flat on a soft blanket while asleep. Measure from top of head down to heels.

2. Chest Circumference

Wrap the soft tape comfortably around the widest part of their chest directly below underarms.

Between Sizes?

We always advise sizing up. Little cubs sprout rapidly, and rolled cuffs on our waffle knits look adorably intentional!

Cloth Diaper Friendly

Our bottom cuts feature a wider saddle gusset, seamlessly accommodating both disposable and fluffy reusable cloth diapers.

Pre-Washed & Pre-Shrunk

Every garment undergoes gentle organic bio-washing. No post-wash surprises or shrinkage when laundered as directed.
