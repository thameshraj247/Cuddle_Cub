Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to reveal detailed care, fitting, and shipping guidance.

Are your buttons and snaps choke-safe for active infants?

Yes, absolutely! Every single button on our rompers and festive pieces is cross-stitched with industrial-grade high-tensile security thread and undergoes rigorous 90-newton pull testing. Our metal snaps are nickel-free and hypoallergenic.

Are all fabrics certified 100% GOTS organic and dye-free?

Yes! Every cotton fiber is GOTS-certified (Global Organic Textile Standard) from soil to finished seam. We strictly use non-toxic, closed-loop botanical earth dyes and zero heavy metal fixatives, safeguarding sensitive newborn skin against irritation.

How fast do orders ship, and is the packaging plastic-free?

All in-stock orders are carefully wrapped and dispatched within 24 business hours. We operate 100% plastic-free fulfillment: our mailers are FSC-certified recycled kraft paper and seal with biodegradable cornstarch adhesive.

What is your exchange policy if a size doesn't fit my baby?

We provide 30 days of 100% free fit exchanges with zero return shipping fees. If your baby hits a sudden growth spurt or the size isn't ideal, message our concierge team and we will express-ship the replacement size immediately.

Can I book an in-person bespoke styling appointment?

Yes, our Portland Flagship Boutique offers complimentary private 45-minute Layette & Festive consultations. You will have access to private fitting suites, fabric swatches, and custom monogramming guidance with our senior stylists.

Do you offer complimentary heirloom gift boxes and notes?

Every order can include our signature unboxing kit with embossed tissue, dried organic lavender sachet, and a personalized wax-sealed greeting card handwritten with your bespoke message at no additional charge.
